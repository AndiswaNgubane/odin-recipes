An omlette is an simple egg dish filled with vegetables

Ingredients

  • Eggs
  • Bacon
  • Cheese
  • Onion
  • Bell peppers
  • Olive oil
  • Mushrooms
  • Avocado

Steps

  1. Saute Onions and Bell peppers and vegetables with curry & paprika
  2. Beat your eggs on a seperate bowl
  3. On a separate pan cook your egg and set
  4. Add sauted vegetables to egg and top with cheese
  5. Fold the omlette and serve
