Lasagna is an Italian dish made with pasta sheets, mince and sauces.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • Minced beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Bell peppers
  • Olive oil
  • Cheese

Steps

  1. Saute Onions and Bell peppers
  2. Add beef mince and cook with spices of your choice untill well done
  3. Layer pasta, cooked beef mince, and sauce
  4. Top with grated cheese
  5. Bake until golden
