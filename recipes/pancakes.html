Pancakes are sofy fluffy cakes made from batter

Ingredients

  • Pancake easy mix
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Mix eggs and milk with oil.
  2. In a seperate bowl, mix egg and milk mixture with easy mix and stir until smooth.
  3. Heat butter in a pan.
  4. Pour some pancake batter and cook till bubbles form.
  5. Flip the pancake and cook the other side.
  6. Serve with your favourite toppings.
